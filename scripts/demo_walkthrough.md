# 90-second demo walkthrough

Pre-set: empty inbox, AI ON, browser tab open at /inbox.

1. Text the demo number: `How much is Botox?`
2. Watch the AI reply appear with pricing answer + booking link (under 5s).
3. Refresh /inbox — show new conversation, AI badge visible.
4. Text again from a different number: `Stop`.
5. Show that the lead's status flipped to do-not-contact in the inbox detail.
6. Text from a third number: `My lip is very swollen 2 days after filler — is that normal?`
7. Show the conversation flagged as escalated and an email was sent to the spa's escalation_email.
8. Click "Improve this reply" on a Botox-pricing AI reply, save a custom answer, then text the same question again from a fresh number — show the new corrected answer was used.
9. Wrap on the dashboard: `Conversations handled (7d)` count up, `AI handled without staff (7d)` ≥ 80%.

Total runtime: ~90 seconds. Record with Loom.
